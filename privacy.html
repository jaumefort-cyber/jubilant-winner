Google API Services User Data — Limited Use Disclosure. The Application’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. What Google user data the Application accesses

When you connect the Application to your Google account it requests access to the following data, and only this data:

  • Your Google Drive file metadata and file contents (files and folders within your own Google Drive). This is used solely to list, read, copy, move, and synchronize files between your Google Drive and the destinations you explicitly choose (for example, a local folder on your own computer or another storage destination you configure).
  • Your Google account OAuth tokens (an OAuth 2.0 access token and refresh token). These tokens allow the Application to act on your behalf for the operations you run, and nothing else.

The Application does not request access to your Gmail, contacts, photos (Picasa/Google Photos), calendar, or any other Google service beyond Google Drive.

2. How the Application uses Google user data

Google user data is used exclusively to provide and improve the core functionality of the Application:

  • Listing, reading, downloading, uploading, moving, and syncing files between Google Drive and destinations you configure.
  • Maintaining sync state (for example, metadata about which files have already been transferred) so that operations are efficient.

We do not use Google user data for any other purpose. Without limiting the foregoing, we do not:

  • Sell Google user data or transfer it to data brokers or information resellers.
  • Use Google user data for advertising, targeted advertising, retargeting, interest-based advertising, or determining credit-worthiness or lending purposes.
  • Use Google user data to build databases of user information.
  • Use Google Drive data (including via Google Workspace APIs) to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models, personalized or non-personalized.

3. Where your data is stored

  • File contents you sync are transferred directly between Google and the destination you choose (typically your own local computer). They are not stored on any server operated by us.
  • OAuth tokens and sync state (for example, cached metadata needed to detect changed files) are stored only on your own local machine, in the Application’s configuration directory. They are never uploaded to us.

4. Sharing and transfer of Google user data

We do not share, transfer, sell, rent, or disclose Google user data to any third party, except:

  • Where the operation itself requires it and you have explicitly configured it (for example, when you sync your Google Drive to another cloud storage provider, the data necessarily passes through or to that provider, under your instruction).
  • Where required by law, in which case we will notify you when legally permitted to do so.

5. Data protection and security

The following measures are in place to protect the confidentiality and integrity of your Google user data:

  • All transfers with Google use TLS encryption (HTTPS).
  • OAuth tokens are stored on your local machine only, protected by standard operating-system file permissions.
  • The Application never transmits your data to us: there is no intermediate server operated by the developer.
  • You can revoke the Application’s access at any time by removing it from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which invalidates the tokens.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • OAuth tokens are retained on your local machine for as long as the Application remains authorized in your Google account configuration. You can delete them at any time by (a) revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions and (b) deleting the Application’s configuration folder on your computer.
  • Sync state and cached metadata are retained for as long as the Application is installed and configured, and are deleted when you remove the configuration or uninstall the Application.
  • File contents synced to your chosen destinations are retained on those destinations under your control.

Because we do not operate servers that store your data, we hold no copies of your Google user data and there is nothing for us to delete centrally. You may also request deletion of any data by contacting us at the email address above; we will confirm in writing that no such data is retained by us.

7. Your choices and control

  • You can grant or revoke the Application’s Google Drive access at any time from your Google Account security settings.
  • You can see exactly which scopes the Application requests before granting consent.
  • You choose every destination your data is synced to.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the Application accesses, uses, stores, or shares Google user data, we will update this policy, change the “Last updated” date, and, where required under the Google API Services User Data Policy, seek your consent before those changes take effect. The current version is always available at the URL where this policy is hosted.
